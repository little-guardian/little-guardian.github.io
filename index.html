Built to protect, not to watch everything

Your family's data stays your family's.

Little Guardian was designed around one rule: collect only what keeps someone safe, and put every control in the hands of the people who love them.

◐

No public visibility

Location and vitals are visible only to the guardians and caregivers you approve. They are never public and never sold.

◑

Every change, logged

Geofence edits, monitoring toggles, and PIN attempts are all recorded, so nothing changes without a trace.

◒

You hold the PIN

Monitoring can only be paused by whoever holds the PIN. Not the device, not the person wearing it, and not us.
