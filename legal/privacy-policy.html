Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

We collect only the information needed to run Little Guardian, support guardians, and keep monitored people safer.

Information we may collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, and approved caregiver roles.
  • Safety data such as device status, live location, safe place activity, heart rate readings, alerts, and PIN events.
  • Support messages and basic technical logs that help us troubleshoot the service.

How we use information

We use information to provide alerts, display safety status, maintain the service, prevent misuse, and respond to requests. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can request access, correction, or deletion by using the data deletion request page or by contacting us at hello@littleguardian.app.
