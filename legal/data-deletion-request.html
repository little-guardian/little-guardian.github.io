Data Deletion Request

Last updated: 23 August 2026

To request deletion of account or safety data, email hello@littleguardian.app from the address linked to your account.

What to include

  • Your full name and account email.
  • The data you want deleted.
  • Whether the request is for you, a dependent, or an organization account.

What happens next

We will verify the request, confirm what can be deleted, and complete eligible deletion as soon as reasonably possible.
